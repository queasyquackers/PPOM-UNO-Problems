Three types of cartilage

–long bones, epiphyseal 
plates, articular surfaces 
of synovial joints

–highly flexible (e.g. auricle 
of ear, epiglottis)

=== PAGE 28 ===
Skeletal muscle
• Voluntary somatic muscle that 
moves or stabilizes structures
• Attached to bone, cartilage, 
ligaments, fascias, organs, skin
• Length: Belly + Tendon
• Named for action, position, 
length, or attachments.
– Examples:
• Adductor pollicis
• Tibialis anterior

=== PAGE 29 ===
A muscle contraction is the activation of 
muscle fibers resulting in the generation of tension.
A length change (in either direction) may occur but is 
not required.

=== PAGE 30 ===
Muscle Action
• The action of a muscle is what 
it does when it contracts and 
shortens with no resistance.
• In real life muscles have 
external resistance.
triceps
biceps

=== PAGE 31 ===
• Skeletal muscles function by contracting
– Tonic – muscle tone
– Isometric - muscle length does not change
– Isotonic- muscle length changes, creates movement
• concentric
• eccentric
