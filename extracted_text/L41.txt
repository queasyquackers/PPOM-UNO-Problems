• The spinal muscular atrophies are a group of inherited 
disorders that cause degeneration of anterior horn cells.
• Most common type is caused by a variation in the 
SMN (survival motor neuron 1).
• There are now 9 recognized types identified. All involve varying 
degrees of weakness
• Clinical presentation varies from early death in infancy 
(type 0, 1A) to walking independently as an adult 
(type 4)

• Herpes viruses (HSV-1, HSV-2 and VZV) reside in the 
dorsal root and trigeminal ganglia.
• VZV causes 2 conditions: the primary infection 
(varicella-chicken pox) and the reactivation infection, 
(herpes zoster-shingles).
• Zoster is a common complication of a prior VZV 
infection. Vesicular lesions occur along a dermatone.
