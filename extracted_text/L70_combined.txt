Weight: should be gaining 20-30g/day  - should double weight by 4 months. Triple by 1 year
Normal FT Babies have 14 days to get back to their birth weight, want NO MORE THAN 10% 
weight loss
Asymmetric vs symmetric SGA, LGA 
HC: Birth 35cm: growth fastest during newborn period - 2cm/month for the 3 months, then 1 
cm/month for next 3 months - slows down to 0.5cm/month for 6 months. 1 year - 
fontanelles fuse 
During this time: also important to check newborn screening results - if normal - can take up 
to 2 weeks to come back
If abnormal: usually clinic gets faxed important and emergent - might need redraw or 
intervention 
Ask about feeding what and how much and how often, ask about elimination and urination, 
always ask about Safe Sleep and how mom and dad are doing. Use Edinburgh Postnatal 
depression scale
Happy mommy = Happy baby

Types of scalp findings from delivery in newborn
Caput succedaneum – like a CAP swelling is 
throughout, crosses suture lines, usually prolonged 
delivery – due to birth canal or vacuum assisted. 
Resolves 24-48 hours
Cephalohematoma – DOES not cross suture lines, 
often with bruising, skin discoloration, Resolves 2-4 
weeks, +can cause jaundice 
Subgleal hemorrhage: dangerous, crosses sutures, 
Can accumulate a lot of blood causing anemia, 
Hypovolemia, +positive fluid wave

➢ Risk Factors: Female, Breech, Pos Family history 
➢ Barlow sign  "Back"
➢ Maneuver attempts to subluxate or dislocate unstable hip 
from acetabulum with gentle hip adduction
➢ Ortolani sign  "Out"
➢ Maneuver attempts to reduce a subluxated or dislocated 
femoral head back into acetabulum with gentle hip 
abduction
➢ Asymmetry
➢ Asymmetrical gluteal or thigh skinfolds
➢ Specificity is low; finding is present in up to 25% of 
unaffected infants
➢ Younger than 4 months – b/l Hip US

Children - toddlers up to 5
Ask milestone questions: Gross motor, Fine motor, 
Language, Social Emotional
Screenings: M-CHAT scoring at 18 months, 24 months
Developmental screening: 9months, 18 months, 30 
months (Ages and Stages Ques) vs surviellance  at each 
well child visit
How is school/daycare going? Changes in social 
situation?
Safety questioning: guns, smoking, seat belts, car 
seats/booster seats, helmets while riding bike, smoke 
detectors, carbon monoxide detectors, poison control 
info at hand for toddlers
Anticipatory guidance: baby proofing house, locking 
away meds, cabinet + oven clamps  
Hearing and Vision screenings in clinic – older than 3-4 years of 
age
AAP recommends Lead and Iron testing at 1 yr plus/minus 2 
yr ( pre-mie/WIC 9 months), BP measurement starting at age 3
T oilet Training: usually >18 months when child is ready
