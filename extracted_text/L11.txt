Source: Course Syllabus, CPG
At the end of this lecture, students will be able to:
1. Describe the gross anatomy of the hypothalamus, 
including major hypothalamic nuclei.
2. Describe the functions of the hypothalamus, including 
hypothalamic hormone secretion and its regulation of 
pituitary hormones.
3. Understand clinical correlations between the location 
of hypothalamic nuclei and their dysfunction.
4. Describe the anatomy and regulation of feeding 
behavior and clinical correlation to obesity.

SUMMARY
Hypothalamic 
Nucleus/Area
Function
Anterior Hypothalamic Nucleus
Temperature regulation through cooling
Posterior Hypothalamic Nucleus
Temperature regulation through conservation and production of heat
Supraoptic Nucleus
AVP/ADH and oxytocin synthesis
Paraventricular Nucleus
AVP/ADH, CRF, TRH, and oxytocin synthesis; projections to autonomic nuclei
Medial Preoptic Nucleus
Regulation of gonadotropic hormone secretion
Arcuate Nucleus
Production of stimulatory or inhibitory hypothalamic factors, feeding
Dorsomedial Nucleus
Role in aggressive behavior, reproduction, feeding behavior, stress response
Suprachiasmatic Nucleus
Regulation of circadian rhythms
Mammillary Nuclei
Input from hippocampus, projections to thalamus; important in episodic 
memory
Ventromedial Nucleus
Satiety center, synthesizes somatostatin
Lateral Hypothalamic Nucleus
Feeding center, arousal center
