Early Symptoms of ASD

--- SLIDE 23 ---
• Early diagnosis and intervention is optimal
• History
• Assess hearing and vision
• Consider comorbid conditions
• Screening tools used by PCP
• Infant-Child Checklist: < 18 months 
• M-CHAT-R: 18 – 30 months
• Child Autism Screening test: preschool/school age
• Refer for in depth evaluation by Developmental Pediatrician and multidisciplinary 
team
• Do not delay management steps while awaiting this evaluation and formal diagnosis
• Refer to appropriate services ASAP
Diagnosing ASD

--- SLIDE 49 ---
Summary Slide
• Normal child development is variable
• Delays can occur in any developmental domain
• It is important to monitor closely for developmental 
delay and neurodevelopmental disorders because 
intervening early is key
• Always consider a broad differential diagnosis and 
comorbid conditions
• A thorough history is key
• Education and support for parents is very important
