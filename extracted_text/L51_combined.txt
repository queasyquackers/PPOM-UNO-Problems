Course Syllabus
• 1) Understand how to evaluate memory loss and 
compare dementia to delirium.
• 2) Describe proper history taking in the setting of 
memory loss.
• 3) Generate a differential diagnosis for memory loss.
• 4) Consider reversible causes of memory loss.
• 5) Apply appropriate treatment options (if they 
exist).

Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders- 5th Edition
• Disturbance of consciousness with reduced ability to 
focus
• Develops over a short period of time (hours-days)
• Fluctuates during the course of the day. There is an 
additional disturbance in memory, disorganization, 
language, visuospatial ability or perception.
• Generally reversible
