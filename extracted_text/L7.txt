=== PAGE 2 ===
Session objectives
1) Students will be able to understand the divisions of the medulla, 
        pons, and midbrain from external views and transverse sections.
2) Students will be able to identify the major structures, reflecting nuclei and 
tracts, on the external anatomy of the brainstem, along with cranial 
nerves and ventricles. 
3) Students will be able to identify the major nuclei on transverse sections of 
the brainstem and cerebellum.
4) Students will be able to describe the placement of the cranial nerves on 
the brainstem.

=== PAGE 30 ===
Summary
• The brainstem has distinctive external features for the medulla, pons, 
and midbrain.
• The tectum is in the dorsal midbrain only. Tegmentum is further ventral 
and at all divisions. And the basilar division is most anterior/ventral and 
present at all levels. 
• Distinct nuclei are present at each level of the brainstem.
• There is a rational order for numbering the cranial nerves.
