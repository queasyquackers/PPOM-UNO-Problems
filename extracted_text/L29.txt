=== PAGE 2 ===
Session Objectives
•
Students will be able to describe the basic mechanisms of neuronal proliferation in the fetal 
ventricular zones and how this relates to neocortical size and disorders such as 
microcephaly. 
•
Students will be able to describe the basic principles of neuronal migration in the fetal 
ventricular zone, subventricular zone, and cortical plate and how this relates to the 
establishment of the neocortical lamina.
•
Students will be able to describe the mechanisms underlying disorders of neuronal migration 
such as subcortical band heterotopia, lissencephaly, etc.
•
Students will be able to describe the basic organization of the formation of sulci and gyrii in 
the developing neocortex.

Neuronal Production – proliferation of neurons in order to 
appropriately populate the brain.
Neuronal Placement – the migration of neurons to their 
appropriate laminar and areal position.
Neuronal Performance – intrinsic neuronal physiology (ion 
channels & transmitter receptors), synaptic physiology of 
networks of neurons
THE 3P’s ORGANIZE AND CONTROL BEHAVIOR:
sensation & perception, cognition & memory, motor 
control, sleep/wake, emotion, etc.

=== PAGE 27 ===
Neuronal Migration & Placement
• Radial glia (RG) fibers contact the pial surface forming a 
substrate onto which newborn neurons can adhere & 
migrate in an inside-outside pattern.
• Migrating neurons must recognize the molecular cues that 
instruct the direction, distance, and destination of their 
journey. Intracellular proteins like microtubule associate 
proteins contribute to shape change and movement 
capabilities of neurons to reach their appropriate layer. 
• Deficits in migration cause heterotopia and lissencephaly.
