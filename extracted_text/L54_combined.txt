American Stroke Association
• A transient ischemic attack is a 
temporary blockage of blood flow to the 
brain.
• The clot usually dissolves on its own or 
gets dislodged. Symptoms usually last 
less than 5 minutes, but can last up to 24 
hours. 
• A TIA can help prevent a stroke, as it is a 
warning.
TIA

--- Page 32 ---
American Stroke Association
• Stroke mimics: Migraine, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, 
peripheral nerve entrapment, brain hemorrhage (i.e. 
subdural hematoma).
• Motor and speech symptoms have a higher 
likelihood of brain ischemia as the cause of the 
symptoms.
• About 20% of patients presenting with TIAs will have 
a stroke within the next 90 days. Highest risk is 
within the next 24 hours.
