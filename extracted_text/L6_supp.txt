Label or add the following:
Grey matter:
1. Dorsal horn 
2. Intermediate gray 
3. Ventral horn 
White matter:
4. Spinothalamic tract 
5. Lateral corticospinal tract 
6. Posterior (dorsal) column 
Function of each:
1. Dorsal horn = Somatosensory, 2° neurons Pain & Temp
2. Intermediate gray = Sympathetics in Thoracic & Lumbar
3. Ventral horn = Lower motor neurons (LMNs)
4. Spinothalamic tract = Axons of 2° neurons for Pain and Temp
5. Lateral corticospinal tract = Axons of upper motor neurons (UMNs)
6. Posterior (dorsal) column = Primary afferents for touch, vibration and proprioception
