Source: Course Syllabus
1.
Compare and contrast Gram positive and Gram-negative bacterial 
cell envelop
2.
Identify and state the importance and significance of the Gram 
stain as a bacteriological tool
3.
Identify exceptions to the nomenclature of the Gram reaction (i.e. 
Wall-less bacteria, Acid-fast bacteria)
4.
Determine the role of virulence factors such as capsules and LPS 
(endotoxin) in the pathogenesis of bacterial infections
5.
Identify bacterial endospores and distinguish their unique 
properties
6.
Identify and distinguish between medically significance spore-
forming bacteria
7.
Distinguish between different modes of horizontal gene transfer in 
bacteria and the significance in a clinical setting
8.
Distinguish the different growth environments of bacteria and the 
significance in a clinical setting
