=== PAGE 6 ===
Glucocorticoids
• Stimulate synthesis of lipocortin
• Lipocortin inhibits Phospholipase A2
• Blocks starting point for prostaglandin, leukotriene synthesis

=== PAGE 7 ===
Glucocorticoids
HPA suppression: taper dose slowly

• -momab  =  mouse (murine)

=== PAGE 10 ===
Dimethyl fumarate
• Nrf2 activator via 
metabolite monomethyl 
fumarate.
• AE:  GI upset, flushing, 
Progressive multifocal 
leukoencephalopathy 
(PML), elevated liver 
enzymes, increased 
infection risk. 

=== PAGE 11 ===
Fingolimod
• Sequestering of lymphocytes
• AE: Headache, bradycardia, 
activation of infections, PML.
• Basal cell carcinoma
• Test VZV antibody
• Evaluate EKG (at first dose, re-
initiation).

=== PAGE 12 ===
Glatiramer acetate
• AE: Injection site reactions
• Post-injection reaction: flush, 
SOB, palpitations ~occurs 
once.
• Lipoatrophy (SQ injection 
effect)
Why infusion reactions?
How to manage infusion 
reactions?

=== PAGE 13 ===
Interferon B1a
• Cytokine, Via JAK-STAT 
pathway
• MS: inhibits T-cell 
activation and migration 
across BBB. 
• AE: flu-like symptoms, 
depression, injection 
reactions, anemia.
• Also treats hepatitis B, C.

=== PAGE 14 ===
Natalizumab
• Binds (a4, b1) receptor, stops 
T-lymphocyte adhesion to 
VCAM
• AE: infusion reactions, PML, 
hepatotoxicity,  infection risk
• Also used for Crohn’s disease

=== PAGE 17 ===
Mitoxantrone: Secondary Progressive 
MS
• Intercalating agent
• “Doxorubicin” like. Anthracycline
• DNA strand scission via effects 
on topoisomerase II
• Binding to membranes alters 
fluidity
• Generation of oxygen radicals
• AE: cardio, N/V, alopecia, 
immunosuppression
• Also treats acute myeloid 
leukemia (AML), prostate, breast 
cancer

=== PAGE 18 ===
• Synthetic deoxyadenosine (purine) analog. 
Antipyrimidine
• Antimetabolite chemotherapeutic (treats hairy 
cell leukemia)
• Greater effect on B cells vs T cells; allows for 
apoptosis of lymphocytes.
• AE: Lymphopenia and infections (VZV)
