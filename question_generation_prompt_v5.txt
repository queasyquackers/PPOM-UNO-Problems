**CRITICAL - STEP/COMLEX Style Vignettes:**
You MUST write questions that mimic the length and detail of actual board exams.
- **Vignette Structure (REQUIRED):**
    - **Demographics:** Age, Gender.
    - **Chief Complaint:** Why are they here?
    - **HPI (History of Present Illness):** Duration, quality, severity, timing, context.
    - **PMH/PSH/Meds:** Relevant history and medications (include distractors if appropriate).
    - **Vitals:** T, BP, HR, RR, O2 sat (use realistic values).
    - **Physical Exam:** Inspection, palpation, auscultation, special tests.
    - **Diagnostic Data:** Labs, imaging, OMM findings (if applicable).
- **Length:** Vignettes should be **4-8 sentences** on average for Board Style questions.

**CRITICAL - Question Mix Strategy (50/50 Split):**
To ensure a high-yield exam that balances depth with breadth, you MUST follow this distribution:
1.  **Type A - Full Clinical Vignette (50%):** 4-8 sentences. Full demographics, HPI, Vitals, Physical Exam findings. used for 2nd/3rd order diagnosis and management.
2.  **Type B - Rapid Concept Check (50%):** 2-3 sentences. Shorter, focused clinical context or direct anatomy/physiology application. Used to test specific facts (axes, landmarks, attachments).

**CRITICAL - Consistency & Interleaving:**
- You **MUST** interleave these types (e.g., A, B, A, B...).
- **DO NOT** front-load the vignettes and let the quality drop at the end.
- The 35th question must be as high-quality as the 1st.
- **Osteopathic Connection:** Continue to include structural exam findings in both types where relevant.

**CRITICAL - "Rule-Out" Explanations (Review Mode Compatible):**
For **INCORRECT** answer choices, you **MUST** follow this strict format to support the "Global Review" mode:
1.  Start with "Incorrect." and explain why it doesn't fit the current case.
2.  Follow with: "**This would be the correct answer if...**" and describe the specific scenario where this option would be right.

> *Example:* "Incorrect. Vancomycin inhibits cell wall synthesis. This would be the correct answer if the patient had an MRSA infection requiring coverage against Gram-positive cocci."

For the **CORRECT** answer choice:
1.  Start with "Correct." and provide a comprehensive explanation.
